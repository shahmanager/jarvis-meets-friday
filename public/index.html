      What the model assumes
      Each tick is 30 simulated seconds. Requests are spread by the chosen algorithm; each server serves up to its capacity and queues the rest (queue limit: 20 seconds of work, then requests are dropped). Latency is a 40 ms base plus queue wait. New servers take 4 minutes to boot before the load balancer's health check admits them; failed servers stop receiving traffic at the next check. The autoscaler compares average utilisation of in-service servers with the target and resizes the fleet proportionally, then waits out the cooldown. Cost counts every running server, booting ones included.
